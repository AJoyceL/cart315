### Sept 20

Brainstorming for ideas isn't the easiest *skill* for me, I often struggle coming up with ideas within a timely manner, but somehow, I manage. 
I was mulling over what variation I wanted to make. About what I could do to keep it simple enough to code and somewhat enjoyable to play.
And like that, the first thing - confusion - that came to mind. Much like what I was feeling. I ended up wanting to create a variation that felt confusing and/or counterintuitive.

The first change I made - after a few considerations - was to change the scoring and win system. Rather than increasing your points, the goal is to lower the opponent's score from 10 to 0 to win. In this instance, if you score, your opponent's loses a point, bringing them you closer to a win.

### Sept 22

The idea came to me during my doctor's appointment, while I was waiting for my name to be called. The wait was long and boring, my 10am appointment ended up starting at 10h43.
I thought: "Wait! What if the ball dissapeared?! Like my time, right now, in this waiting room." 
What if I turned the ball invisible for a few seconds when the it collides with the paddle?

So, when I got home, ate, fed the dogs and watched my FFAR248 lecture, I jumped on Unity while my idea was somewhat still fresh. 
The second change, as I had decided, was inverted controls. It was simple enough to apply, switch the W and S keys and test it. It works just I had intended, adaptable, but counterintuitive when you expect the W key to go up and the S kew to go down. 
I ended up getting confused myself :(

Now for the third chnage.
I understood the concept of making the ball invisible with alpha, duration and collision, but putting them together was another battle in itself. Luckily, I have something called the *Internet* to look for references. 
With my [reference](https://discussions.unity.com/t/changing-alpha-of-2d-game-object-when-collided-with/1536496/3) and the script Vadim already gave us, I slowly worked and struggled my way into something that didn't crash. 
It was as frsutrating - thinking I had something, then realising it didn't work when I was testing my variation - as it was satisfying. The sudden burst of satisfaction and joy I felt when I finally got it to work. 

It might not be my finest work, but I feel satisfied enough to leave it at that.
