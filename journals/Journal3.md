### Oct 25

Created a new Unity file for my Protoryoe 3. I called it walking for now, but I'm looking for ideas on the itchio link we received.
A few thigns that caught my attention was [*The Black Within*](https://waleedzo.itch.io/the-black-within) by waleedzo, [*Liminal Gallery*](https://kenforest.itch.io/liminal-gallery) by kenforest and [*POOLS*](https://tensori.itch.io/pools) by tensori.

Since I don't have time to play them, I'm taking inspirationfrom the video clips/trailers and pictures shown in their galley. 

*The Black Within* is th efirst one that caught my interest because of the dark and psychological-horror atmosphere. I'm a big fan of horror survival games, including more atmospheric games like *The Morturary Assistant*, *Until Dawn* and *Alien Isolation*. 
Horror games were a big part of my childhood, so I'm more inclined to work on a project leaning towards the horror theme/genre.

I was attracted to *Liminal Gallery* and *POOLS* for the liminal and uncany atmosphere. I'm familiar with *POOLS*, a game inspired by *The Backrooms* by Kane Parsons. I like the isolation and surrealism liminal games create, although I won't be making anythign as extensive as these inspirations, I want to try and capture the atmosphere.

I built a looping maze without any dead ends to mimic the uncanny feeling of a liminal room. It seems a bit large at the moment, but I'll adjust the map size as I go: deleting walls and resize the overall size. I'll add materials, textures, lighting, sound and more tomorrow and over the days to at least have something presentable.
