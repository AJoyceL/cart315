What I understood from the *The Anatomy of Prototypes: Prototypes as Filters, Prototypes as Manifestations of Design Ideas* is that prototypes help designers experiment with ideas rather than focusing on the final design. It explains how prototypes help explore different ideas, expand and rework details, and understand what works before reaching the final product. 

As an artist, I can relate to this process through my sketching process. When designing a character, I usually sketch multiple concepts without fully finishing any of them, and still draw more concepts of the same character, because I might like the hairstyle of one concept, the outfit in another, and the pose of a third. Instead of choosing one sketch, I combine the elements I like for the final concept. Sometimes, I feel like I’m walking in circles by reworking the same idea multiple times instead of perfecting it. This reading helped me understand that this struggle in an important part of the creative process of designing. Every sketch helps me understand what I want even if I don’t use every one.

I realised how I sometimes pressure myself o perfect my ideas from the beginning, focusing on making them look good too early on. I always want my sketches to look clean even when I’m still figuring out my art style and the design. This reminded me that I don't need to know what I want from the beginning, I can discover it throughout the process.
